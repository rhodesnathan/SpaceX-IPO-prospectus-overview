Company History
Key Milestones
2002
SpaceX founded by Elon Musk. Mission: make humanity multiplanetary.
2008
First private company to reach orbit with a liquid-fuel rocket (Falcon 1, 4th attempt).
2012
Dragon first private vehicle to dock with the ISS. NASA Commercial Resupply contract active.
2015
First successful propulsive landing of an orbital-class booster. Established minimum 10-year lead over competitors.
2019
First Starlink satellites launched. World's largest LEO broadband constellation deployment begins.
2020
Dragon carries first crewed ISS mission. Starlink opens commercial broadband service.
2023
Starship first launched. xAI founded, releases Grok-1. SpaceX launches 80%+ of global mass to orbit.
2025
10.3M Starlink subscribers. COLOSSUS II online (1 GW compute). Revenue $18.7B. xAI burns $6.4B in operating losses.
2026
xAI acquired. Prospectus filed May 20. $135/share set June 3. Trading begins June 12 as SPCX. Starship commercial payload delivery targeted H2 2026.
2027
$20B bridge loan maturity. V3 Starlink deployment begins (requires Starship). Starlink Mobile V2 planned.
2028+
Orbital AI compute first deployment. Lunar presence objectives. Bulk of long-term growth thesis begins here. None generating revenue today.
