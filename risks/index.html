What Could Make This Fail
  • Critical
    Starship Dependency
    The entire long-term growth thesis requires Starship at full commercial reusability and high cadence. It has not achieved this. Current rockets cannot deploy V3 or V2 Mobile satellites. If Starship slips, the 95x revenue multiple is very hard to defend.
  • Critical
    $20B Bridge Loan — September 2027
    $20B outstanding, maturing ~15 months post-IPO. Must be refinanced or repaid. If capital markets tighten or the stock underperforms, refinancing terms could be severely adverse.
  • Critical
    Governance Concentration
    Musk controls the vote via Class B. At $1.77T you are betting on one person's judgment, focus, and continued commitment to this company specifically.
  • Significant
    AI Segment is a Deep Cash Furnace
    $12.7B capex and $6.4B operating loss from xAI in 2025. Orbital AI compute not expected to generate revenue before 2028. Starlink funds all of this.
  • Significant
    Regulatory Surface Area
    FAA, FCC, 164-country telecom regulators, EU AI law, FTC child safety inquiries, AI content litigation. Wide simultaneous exposure across three distinct regulated industries.
  • Significant
    Thin Float — Violent Price Swings Expected
    Float ~3–4% of total shares. Morningstar projects 20–30% swings vs Tesla's 10–15%. Limited liquidity to exit if early sentiment turns negative.
  • Monitor
    Potential SpaceX / Tesla Merger
    CNBC reported late May 2026 that Musk discussed a merger with colleagues. No formal announcement. If executed, an unprecedented transaction decided unilaterally by Musk.
